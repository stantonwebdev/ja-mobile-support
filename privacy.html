Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the JA Mobile Services mobile application ("App"), an internal business tool developed exclusively for use by JA Mobile Services personnel.

Information We Collect

The App stores the following data entered by authorized users:

  • Customer names and contact information
  • Vehicle details (make, model, year, VIN)
  • Service job records and notes

How We Use Your Information

All data is used solely for internal business operations by JA Mobile Services staff. Data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Data Storage

Data is stored locally on the user's device using the device's secure local storage. Users may optionally enable cloud backup to a private Firebase Realtime Database configured and controlled by JA Mobile Services. This backup is encrypted in transit and accessible only to authorized personnel.

Data Retention

Data remains on the device until the user removes it or uninstalls the app. Cloud backup data is retained until manually deleted by the administrator.

Third-Party Services

The App may optionally use Google Firebase for cloud backup purposes. Firebase is governed by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. No other third-party services are used.

Access

This App is restricted to authorized JA Mobile Services employees only. It is not available to the general public.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact your JA Mobile Services administrator.
