JA Mobile Services

Internal mobile app for JA Mobile Services field staff.

Support

This application is for authorized JA Mobile Services personnel only. If you are experiencing issues with the app, please contact your supervisor or system administrator.

Features

  • Customer and vehicle management
  • Service job tracking
  • Invoice generation
  • Cloud backup and sync

Privacy

All data entered into this application is stored locally on your device and optionally backed up to a private Firebase database configured by your administrator. No data is shared with third parties.

Contact

For technical support, contact your JA Mobile Services administrator at stantonwebdev@gmail.com.
